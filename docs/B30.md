# B30: campus report and matching CSV (REQ-RPT-01, TC-14)

Administrator-only screen at `/accounts/admin-config/reports/` (link on the Administrator home).
Pick a start and end date; the page shows the report and a "Download CSV" link with the same
filters. `accounts/campus_reports.py` computes every figure once (`build_report`); the page and
`report_to_csv` both read that one result, so the CSV cannot differ from the screen.

## Figures
Volumes by category, location and department; total; open, overdue, escalated and reopened
complaints; total reopens (authorized reopen information); mean and median resolution hours.

## D03 rules: PROVISIONAL until the team confirms
All in one block at the top of `campus_reports.py`; changing a rule changes a constant, not the logic.
- Dataset: complaints submitted in the selected dates. Both days are included, as whole days in the campus time zone.
- Open: Submitted, Assigned, In progress or Reopened (not Resolved, Closed or Rejected).
- Overdue: open and past its due time when the report is generated.
- Resolution time: resolved time minus submitted time, only when resolved is later than submitted; others are excluded and counted. A reopened complaint uses its latest resolved time.
- Range limit: at most 366 days (NFR-PERF-02 covers 12 months).

## Privacy and access
- Only aggregates are read: no submitter, reference digest, complaint ID, title, description or evidence. Anonymous complaints are counted, never attributed (REQ-ANON-02).
- Administrator only. Heads, Staff and Complainants get 403; signed-out users go to login. `build_report(..., department=...)` is the shared contract B31 uses for a Head's own department; its caller must authorize with `can_view_report`.
- CSV cells that begin with `= + - @` are prefixed so a spreadsheet reads them as text. The download is `no-store`.
