   320px. Correct it without losing safe fields. Submit valid synthetic evidence

3. Demonstrate the private anonymous flow in an approved isolated environment:
   one-time reference, restricted tracking, confirmation/reopening and maximum
   review behavior. Mask the raw private credential from the recording. Show that
   another department cannot read the complaint/evidence/feedback.
4. Show actual staff queue/allocation and the resulting history/outbox, without
   disclosing reporter identity or private reference. Demonstrate safe stale
   action or recovery behavior using the captured actual test evidence.
5. Present actual Q04 timings, sample/error counts, confirmed conditions and
   pass/fail thresholds. Show the full inventory, including report/upload/
   pagination. Do not use unit-test durations as performance measurements.
6. Present the genuine Q06 observer record: first-time attestation, screen
   sequence/count, assistance, desktop/320px findings, defects and retests. State
   any failed criterion directly.
7. Close on the requirement matrix and source-specific evidence links. Distinguish
   completed implementation/tests from unresolved deployment or acceptance work.
